# Presentations

These presentations document the development of Project90's prosthetic pressure-sensing sock. The public copies are PDF exports. Editable source decks, speaker notes, internal reviews, and implementation details remain private.

- [May 2026 progress update](2026-05-prosthetic-sensor-sock-update.pdf) - the initial four-sensor proof of concept, wireless data pipeline, and early MATLAB visualization.
- [July 2026 lab update](2026-07-prosthetic-smart-sock-lab-update.pdf) - the custom PCB system, 16-channel acquisition, current software, and proposed bench tests.
- [Tech Futures Challenge 2026](2026-tech-futures-prosthetic-sensor-sock.pdf) - the Project90 team presentation covering the problem, prototype, design iterations, and future work.
- [CUBEC presentation](2026-cubec-prosthetic-sock.pdf) - an early project and commercialization pitch, retained as part of the project's history.

The system shown here is a research prototype. It has not been clinically validated and is not presented as a diagnostic device. Any future study involving human participants would require the appropriate institutional ethics review and regulatory planning.

Some slides include third-party photographs, product images, or published research figures for educational and historical context. Rights to those materials remain with their original owners.
